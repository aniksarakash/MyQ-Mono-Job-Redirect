# ✉️ Optional End User Notice

Optional with this design, since nothing is deleted and jobs stay collectable everywhere else. Send it if the customer wants visible communication.

Replace every bracketed placeholder before sending.

---

> **Subject: Change to printing on the Ricoh IM C2010, from [DATE]**
>
> From [DATE], the Ricoh IM C2010 in [LOCATION] will be used for **colour documents only**.
>
> You do not need to change anything about how you print. If you send a document with no colour in it, it simply will not appear on the IM C2010. Collect it from any other printer as usual. Nothing is deleted.
>
> **Please note:** this is based on what is inside your document, not the setting you choose when printing. A document with no colour in it will be redirected even if you select "Colour" in the print dialog.
>
> For help, contact [IT CONTACT].

---

## 📝 Placeholders

| Placeholder | Fill with |
|---|---|
| `[DATE]` | The date `$ENFORCE` is switched to `true` |
| `[LOCATION]` | Floor, room or department where the IM C2010 sits |
| `[IT CONTACT]` | Service desk email or extension |

---

## 🎯 Keep the second note in

The paragraph beginning "this is based on what is inside your document" is the one that prevents tickets. It maps directly to [limitation 8.1](10-limitations.md), which is the only part of this change users can notice and misunderstand.

If the customer wants the email shortened, cut anything else first.

---

## 💬 Service desk one liner

For the customer's first line team, in case a user calls before reading the email:

> The IM C2010 is now for colour documents only. Your job has not been deleted, it is waiting at every other printer. This is based on whether the document contains colour, not on what you selected when printing.
